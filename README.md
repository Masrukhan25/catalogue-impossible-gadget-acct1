# The Up-Pour Teapot

A catalogue photograph of an impossible gadget: a teapot pouring tea **upwards** into a floating cup, with exactly one saucer as the accessory. Bright white seamless studio, clean catalogue framing.

The absurd job reads from the picture alone: the stream of tea defies gravity, rising in a clean arc from the spout to the cup hovering above it — a teapot for drinking against gravity. The teapot stays the hero of the frame, centred on a plain studio background, with realistic light, shadow and ceramic materials.
